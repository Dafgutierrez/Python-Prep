Soundpeats — Share of Voice in Wireless Earbuds

Weekly share of voice across AI shopping engines, tracked at the brand and ASIN level. Built to sketch what an Amazon-Alexa-for-Shopping equivalent of this dashboard would look like.

Every number on this page is simulated — ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity are queried via a mock responder, not their live APIs (no keys configured; see the pipeline's README). Alexa for Shopping / Amazon Rufus is intentionally not included: Amazon has no public API for it, and the only way to query it programmatically means automating the consumer app, which risks breaching Amazon's Conditions of Use. That channel is scoped as data you'd buy from a vendor, not build in-house.

Methodology (mock mode): a prompt bank of real shopping-intent question patterns (budget, use-case, comparison, gift, troubleshooting, purchase-decision) is sent to each engine weekly; each response is scanned against a product catalog by name/alias match; a “mention” increments that product's and brand's share of voice for the run. Share of voice = a product/brand's mentions ÷ total mentions across all tracked products, within that week.
