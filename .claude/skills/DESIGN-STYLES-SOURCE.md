# Design style skills (reference only)

Source: https://github.com/bergside/awesome-design-skills (MIT License, Copyright (c) 2026 Bergside), commit f631a09, copied on 2026-10-26.

Fifteen style presets copied as plain files (no installer run): premium, clean, minimal, refined, sleek, modern, editorial, spacious, professional, bento, power, bold, dramatic, glassmorphism, gradient. Folders are named `design-<style>`.

Decision of Dafne, 2026-10-26: keep only the styles that make pieces look more aesthetic, eye-catching, clean and premium, and they must not affect what Geonos already has.

Where they live and why (Dafne, 2026-10-26): these are for creating separate pieces (LinkedIn creatives, graphics, one-off pages), not for the Geonos website. They live in this repo (Python-Prep), not in the Geonos repo, so they cannot touch the site, the dashboard, the API or the Vercel build.

Rules:
- Use them with `/design-<style>` or when a design task names a look.
- Brand-imitating presets (Claude, Codex, Roku, Sega, Pac-Man, Tetris, Ant, Material, shadcn, Stitch) were left out on purpose.
- LinkedIn pieces never name Geonos (standing rule).
