I asked Google and Bing “how to know if ChatGPT mentions my brand.” Google suggests nothing. Bing splits between brand monitoring and “how to know if someone used ChatGPT.”

My take: the words of this field are still being claimed, and the search engines have not decided what they mean. That is an opening, not a problem.

What I saw (autocomplete, signed out, 28 October 2026):
→ “how to show up in chatgpt”: Google completes with results, searches and “a winning aeo playbook.” Bing mixes in Turnitin, plagiarism checkers and “how to detect chatgpt.”
→ “what is geo”: Google and Bing complete with geocaching, geography and geometry.
→ “what is aeo”: both already complete with “aeo vs seo” and “aeo and geo.”
→ “ai visibility” completes with tools, score, checker and tracker.

The limit: autocomplete changes with place, time and your history, and it is not search volume. It shows that a question exists, not how many people ask it.

So here are the answers to the ones the search engines do not settle:

What is the difference between AEO and GEO?
They are close to the same task: getting an AI to name or cite you when it answers. AEO says answer engines, GEO says generative engines. What matters is what you measure.

How do I show up in ChatGPT?
There is no list to climb. Its search reads your pages with its own crawler (OAI-SearchBot), so it has to be able to get in first, and your page should answer the question in its first lines.

How do I know if ChatGPT mentions my brand?
Ask your customer’s question several times and save each answer with its date. One run proves nothing: in a SparkToro and Gumshoe.ai study (January 2026, 2,961 runs, not peer reviewed), fewer than 1 in 100 runs returned the same list of brands. Log mentions and citations separately.

What I would do this week:
1️⃣ Write your service the way your customer says it, not the way your industry names it.
2️⃣ Type that phrase into Google and Bing and note what they complete. If they complete something else, your word means something else to them.
3️⃣ Answer it on one page, in the first lines, in your customer’s words.
4️⃣ Check again in a month: autocomplete moves.
