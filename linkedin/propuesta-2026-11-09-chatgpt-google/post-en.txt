OpenAI says ChatGPT now handles about one-fifth as many searches as Google. Independent estimates say 7.5% to 12%. Website traffic says 0.21%. Those are three different things.

Adam Fry, who leads ChatGPT consumer product, said at an OpenAI event in New York that ChatGPT's search-like query volume is roughly one-fifth of Google's (reported by 9to5Google and Big Technology on 3 November 2026).

The asterisks first:
- It is OpenAI's own claim. No Google total, period or regions were published, and OpenAI is private, so nobody can verify it.
- OpenAI counts a search each time a prompt makes ChatGPT search the web. That is not the same unit as a query on Google.

Independent estimates are lower, and they are models too. Ahrefs (February 2026) estimated about 12%, counting only prompts that look like searches, using ChatGPT prompt data from July 2025. Counting every prompt, it got 18%. seoClarity (28 October 2026) estimated about 7.5% and calls its figure a modeled comparison, not a measured share.

Visits tell another story. In the same Ahrefs study of 76,000 websites, ChatGPT sent 0.21% of traffic and Google about 40%. Google sends roughly 190 times more visitors.

And Google is not shrinking. Alphabet reported on 28 October that Search and other revenue grew 12% year over year, and Sundar Pichai said query growth was the highest in five years. A fifth of a growing number is not a loss for Google. That last line is my read.

What I take from it: ChatGPT is large and growing, but the exact share is not known, and "queries", "visits" and "market share" are not interchangeable. The trend matters more than the number.
