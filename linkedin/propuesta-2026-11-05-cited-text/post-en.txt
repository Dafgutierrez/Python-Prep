Why does my page rank on Google but never get cited by AI? One documented reason: AI reads more than it quotes.
AI engines quote short passages under their own limits, and a source they saw is not always a source they cite.

→ Claude Platform Docs (checked Nov 4, 2026): web search returns "cited_text: Up to 150 characters of the cited content"
→ Perplexity API Changelog, Oct 2026, Agent API presets: "web_search max_tokens and max_tokens_per_page rise from 2,000 to 4,000."
→ xAI Docs (checked Nov 5, 2026): allowed_domains will "Only search within specific domains (max 5)"
→ xAI Docs (checked Nov 5, 2026): "not every URL in this list will necessarily be directly referenced in the final answer."

Every engine sets different limits. That is why I track retrieved vs cited engine by engine.

What I would do this week:
1. (10 min) Pick your top page. Rewrite each section's key claim as one sentence under 150 characters, with the number and source inside.
2. (2 min) Put the answer in the first screen of the page.
3. (3 min) Start a sheet: same prompts, every month. Log retrieved vs cited per engine.

A note on the sources: these are developer docs, not how consumer apps choose citations. The Claude and xAI pages show no date, so I give the day I checked them.
