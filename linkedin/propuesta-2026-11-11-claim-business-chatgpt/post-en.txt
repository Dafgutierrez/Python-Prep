OpenAI just launched “claim your business” in ChatGPT.

It is called Businesses in ChatGPT, in beta (10 Nov 2026). In Business Hub you can claim your business, manage the information ChatGPT uses about it, and see mentions and website clicks.

My take: this is a way to give ChatGPT your facts, not a ranking button. OpenAI says so itself: “Claiming does not guarantee higher placement or sales.”

What is confirmed:
- Verification by phone or text to the business number, or a DNS record. Reports add email and Shopify.
- A separate identity check (Persona) before you can publish changes or see insights.
- Insights: mentions, website clicks and discovery topics. Clicks do not confirm visits or conversions.
- Business Agents start with eligible ecommerce and online service businesses in the US.
- Local stores and restaurants can only claim an existing US listing.

I could not confirm availability for Mexico or Latin America.

What I would do this week:
1. Check eligibility and who owns your listing. If someone already claimed it, there is a “Dispute ownership” option.
2. Fix your facts first: prices, stock, delivery and return policies.
3. Measure on your side: filter utm_source=chatgpt.com in your analytics (one hands-on test found ChatGPT adds it to its links) and ask “how did you find us?”

Which fact about your business would you correct first?
